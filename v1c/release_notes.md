---

## Overview

This release publishes **Volume 2 — Version C** of *A WEB of Sharing Trust–Responsibility Toward a Trustless Future*.

Version C addresses a specific lifecycle gap in AI governance:  
**how organizations enter runtime governance responsibly, without asserting trust prematurely**.

It provides **operational transition discipline** only and does not modify governance authority, assurance semantics, or scoring logic.

## What Has Changed

- Introduced **formal onboarding discipline** as a governed transition phase.
- Clarified that **containment precedes alignment** at system entry.
- Added **explicit boundaries** preventing:
  - certification-by-association,
  - dataset-based trust inference,
  - policy authority leakage during onboarding.
- Published **Annex X (Policy & Dataset Lineage)** as a companion, outside Version C.
- Added **machine-readable lineage artifacts** (informative).

---

## What Has Not Changed

- No changes to **Volume 2 — Version A** governance authority.
- No changes to **Compliance Star Certificate (CSC)** semantics or scoring.
- No certification, readiness, or regulatory endorsement is introduced.
- No architectural prescriptions are added.

Where ambiguity arises, **Version A remains authoritative**.

## Intended Use

This release is intended for:

- standards exploration and working-draft discussion,
- regulatory and policy consultation,
- academic and comparative analysis, and
- inspection of onboarding-phase governance behavior.

It must **not** be used to:

- assert compliance or certification,
- market trust or readiness,
- substitute for regulatory determination, or
- bypass evidence-based assurance mechanisms.
