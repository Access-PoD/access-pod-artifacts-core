# Public Review Framing Note

## Purpose of this public review

This public review is intended to support **technical, conceptual, and editorial discussion** of the accompanying materials. The documents are shared to invite informed feedback on clarity, structure, terminology, and positioning within the broader AI governance landscape.

The review is exploratory in nature and is not conducted for the purpose of certification, conformity assessment, or regulatory approval.

---

## Materials under review

The public review applies to the following materials:

- **Access PoD Volume 2**, which explores governance mechanisms such as simulation-based risk discovery, runtime policy attachment, and telemetry-derived governance signals.
- The **Access-mode Index**, which organises governance activity into distinct operational postures reflecting different stages of system maturity, operation, and external engagement.

These materials may be reviewed independently or together.

---

## What feedback is encouraged

Reviewers are invited to provide feedback on:

- **Conceptual clarity**  
  Are the ideas, distinctions, and constructs understandable and well-scoped?

- **Terminology and language**  
  Is the language precise, consistent, and aligned with existing standards vocabulary?

- **Structure and organisation**  
  Does the modular and mode-based framing support reasoning about AI governance across contexts?

- **Usefulness for standards exploration**  
  Do the materials assist discussion of governance across the AI lifecycle, including post-deployment considerations?

- **Gaps or ambiguities**  
  Are there areas that would benefit from clearer explanation, additional context, or explicit boundaries?

---

## What feedback is out of scope

To avoid misinterpretation, the following types of feedback are considered out of scope for this review:

- Requests to demonstrate **compliance, certification, or assurance** against specific standards or regulations.
- Evaluations of **deployment readiness, system safety, or operational adequacy** of any particular AI system.
- Interpretations that treat the materials as **normative requirements**, maturity models, or prescriptive checklists.
- Requests for legal or regulatory determinations.

Such topics may be relevant in other contexts but are not the purpose of this public review.

---

## How feedback will be interpreted

Feedback will be treated as **informative input** to improve clarity, robustness, and alignment with standards discourse.  
Comments do not imply endorsement, acceptance, or rejection by any standards body or authority.

Suggestions may be incorporated, deferred, or declined based on scope, consistency, and intent. Where feedback falls outside the stated scope, it may be acknowledged without action.

---

## Transparency and attribution

Unless otherwise stated, feedback may be summarised or referenced in subsequent revisions or discussion materials.  
Personal or organisational attribution will be handled with care and in accordance with the context in which feedback is provided.

---

## Closing note

The intent of this public review is to encourage thoughtful, constructive engagement with emerging approaches to AI governance. Reviewers are thanked for contributing their expertise and perspectives to this discussion.
